Sample 8.0 Writing

Bài mẫu Band 8.0

150 bài Task 2 theo 15 chủ đề — đọc song ngữ Anh – Việt, xem bài chia đoạn ra sao, rồi luyện dịch lại chính bài đó.

Luyện Writing
Chủ đề:
Đang hiện 150 / 150 bài

🎓Giáo dục· Education

  • Bài 112 câuSome people believe that university education should be free for all students. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  • Bài 212 câuSome people think that children should begin learning a foreign language as soon as they start primary school. Others believe this should happen in secondary school. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
  • Bài 314 câuSome people believe that online learning is as effective as traditional classroom learning. Others disagree. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 413 câuSome people say that homework is a waste of time for school children and should be abolished. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  • Bài 513 câuSome people think that schools should teach practical skills such as cooking and managing money, while others believe schools should focus on academic subjects. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 614 câuSome people believe that examinations are the best way to assess students' abilities, while others think there are better methods. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 715 câuSome people believe that unpaid community service should be a compulsory part of high school programmes. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  • Bài 814 câuNowadays many students choose to study at universities in a foreign country. What are the advantages and disadvantages of studying abroad?
  • Bài 912 câuSome people think that boys and girls should be educated in separate schools, while others believe co-educational schools are better. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 1012 câuSome people believe that teachers have a greater influence on a child's intellectual and social development than parents do. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

🌿Môi trường· Environment

  • Bài 113 câuSome people think that individuals can do nothing to improve the environment, and that only governments and large companies can make a difference. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  • Bài 213 câuClimate change is now a major global concern. What are the main causes of this problem, and what measures can be taken to address it?
  • Bài 313 câuSome people believe that plastic bags should be banned completely because of the damage they cause to the environment. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  • Bài 414 câuSome people think that economic development is more important than protecting the environment, while others believe the opposite. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
  • Bài 512 câuSome people say that protecting wild animals is a waste of resources because they have no place in the modern world. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  • Bài 612 câuIn many cities, drivers are charged a fee to enter the city centre in order to reduce traffic and pollution. What are the advantages and disadvantages of this policy?
  • Bài 713 câuSome people believe that countries should invest heavily in renewable energy sources even if they are more expensive than fossil fuels. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  • Bài 814 câuThe amount of household waste produced in many countries is increasing rapidly. What are the causes of this problem, and what can be done to reduce it?
  • Bài 913 câuSome people believe that tourism damages the natural environment, while others argue that it helps to protect it. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
  • Bài 1014 câuSome people think that environmental problems are too big for individual countries to solve, and that international cooperation is the only solution. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

💻Công nghệ· Technology

  • Bài 113 câuSome people believe that technology has made people more isolated and less sociable. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  • Bài 213 câuArtificial intelligence is expected to replace many jobs in the coming decades. Do you think this is a positive or negative development?
  • Bài 314 câuChildren today spend far more time looking at screens than previous generations did. What are the causes of this trend, and what solutions can you suggest?
  • Bài 414 câuSome people believe that social media has more negative effects than positive ones on individuals and society. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  • Bài 511 câuSome people think that governments should strictly limit the amount of personal data that technology companies are allowed to collect. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  • Bài 611 câuSome people believe that printed books will soon disappear because of digital books, while others think printed books will always be popular. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
  • Bài 714 câuOnline shopping is becoming more popular than shopping in physical stores. What are the advantages and disadvantages of this trend?
  • Bài 816 câuSome people think that schools should replace textbooks with tablets and computers, while others believe traditional textbooks are still more effective. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 914 câuAdvances in technology have made it possible for many people to work from home rather than in an office. Do you think this is a positive or negative development?
  • Bài 1013 câuSome people think that space exploration is a waste of money and that the funds should be spent on solving problems on Earth. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

🩺Sức khỏe· Health

  • Bài 112 câuSome people think that governments should be responsible for the health of their citizens, while others believe individuals should take care of their own health. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 215 câuIn many countries, the number of people who are overweight or obese is increasing. What are the causes of this trend, and what measures could be taken to address it?
  • Bài 313 câuSome people believe that prevention is better than cure, and that governments should spend more on promoting healthy lifestyles than on treating illness. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  • Bài 413 câuSome people believe that traditional medicine is more effective than modern medicine, while others think the opposite. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
  • Bài 513 câuMany people today suffer from stress. What are the main causes of stress in modern life, and what can be done to reduce it?
  • Bài 613 câuSome people think that people should be required to pay for their own medical treatment if their illness is caused by an unhealthy lifestyle, such as smoking or overeating. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  • Bài 712 câuSome people say that the best way to improve public health is to increase the number of sports facilities. Others believe this would have little effect and that other measures are required. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 812 câuSome people think that children should be taught about healthy eating and nutrition at school, while others believe this is the responsibility of parents. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 914 câuIn some countries, the average life expectancy is increasing significantly. What are the advantages and disadvantages of this trend for individuals and society?
  • Bài 1014 câuMental health problems are becoming increasingly common, especially among young people. Why is this happening, and what can be done to address the issue?

💼Công việc· Work

  • Bài 114 câuSome people believe that it is better to work for a large company, while others prefer small businesses. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.
  • Bài 212 câuSome people think that job satisfaction is more important than job security, while others believe that a stable job is more important. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 314 câuIn many countries, people are working longer hours than ever before. What are the causes of this trend, and what effects does it have on individuals and society?
  • Bài 413 câuSome people believe that companies should provide employees with more flexibility in their working arrangements. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  • Bài 513 câuSome people say that money is the most important motivating factor for employees. Others believe that other factors are more important. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 613 câuSome people think that it is better to change jobs frequently, while others believe that staying with one employer for a long time is preferable. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 713 câuSome people believe that unpaid internships exploit young people, while others think they provide valuable experience. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 814 câuNowadays, many people choose to be self-employed rather than to work for a company. Why might this be the case? What could be the disadvantages of being self-employed?
  • Bài 913 câuSome people think that the retirement age should be raised because people are living longer. Others believe that older workers should retire to make way for younger ones. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 1011 câuSome people believe that a person's job title and salary are the best indicators of their success, while others think success should be measured differently. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

✈️Du lịch· Travel

  • Bài 114 câuInternational tourism has become one of the largest industries in the world. Do you think the advantages of tourism outweigh its disadvantages?
  • Bài 212 câuSome people believe that travelling to different countries is the best way to learn about other cultures, while others think that this can be achieved through books, films and the internet. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 312 câuSome people think that young people should travel and see the world before starting university or a career. Others believe this is a waste of time and money. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 415 câuIn many tourist destinations, local residents suffer from the negative effects of tourism. What problems does tourism cause for local people, and what can be done to solve them?
  • Bài 511 câuSome people think that air travel should be restricted because of the pollution it causes, while others believe that people should be free to fly as often as they wish. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 613 câuSome people prefer to travel to places they have never been, while others prefer to return to the same destinations. Discuss both preferences and give your own opinion.
  • Bài 712 câuSome people believe that visitors to other countries should follow local customs and behaviour. Others disagree and think that the host country should welcome cultural differences. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 814 câuNowadays, many people prefer to book their holidays online rather than through travel agents. What are the advantages and disadvantages of this trend?
  • Bài 914 câuSome people think that travelling alone is the best way to travel, while others prefer to travel with friends or family. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 1013 câuSome people believe that governments should spend money on developing tourism, while others think this money should be spent on other public services. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

🍲Ẩm thực· Food

  • Bài 113 câuIn many countries, traditional foods are being replaced by international fast food. Some people think this has negative effects on families and society. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  • Bài 211 câuSome people think that governments should impose a tax on unhealthy foods such as sugary drinks and junk food. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  • Bài 313 câuSome people believe that everyone should adopt a vegetarian diet because it is better for health and the environment. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  • Bài 412 câuSome people think that cooking skills should be taught in schools, while others believe this is unnecessary. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 513 câuMany people today buy food that is produced in other countries rather than locally grown food. What are the advantages and disadvantages of this trend?
  • Bài 615 câuA large proportion of the food produced in the world is wasted. What are the causes of this problem, and what solutions can you suggest?
  • Bài 711 câuSome people believe that organic food is worth its higher price, while others think it is not. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 813 câuIn some countries, people are eating more meals outside the home instead of cooking at home. Why is this happening, and is it a positive or negative development?
  • Bài 913 câuSome people think that the best way to solve the problem of hunger in the world is to increase food production through modern technology, such as genetically modified crops. Others believe there are better solutions. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 1013 câuSome people say that advertising encourages people to buy unhealthy food and that such advertising should be banned. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

👪Gia đình· Family

  • Bài 114 câuIn many countries, the traditional family structure is changing, with more single-parent families and smaller households. What are the causes of this change, and what effects does it have on society?

  • Bài 313 câuSome people think that parents should be strict with their children, while others believe a more relaxed approach is better. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 412 câuIn some countries, more and more elderly people are living in care homes rather than with their families. Do you think this is a positive or negative development?
  • Bài 512 câuSome people think that both parents should share equal responsibility for raising children, while others believe that mothers are naturally better suited to this role. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 613 câuSome people believe that children who grow up in families with many siblings have advantages over only children. Others think being an only child is better. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 713 câuNowadays, many parents give their children a great deal of freedom, while others believe that children should be closely supervised. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 812 câuSome people think that it is better for children to grow up in the countryside than in a big city. To what extent do you agree or disagree?
  • Bài 914 câuIn many countries, young people are choosing to marry and have children later in life than previous generations did. What are the reasons for this trend, and is it a positive or negative development?
  • Bài 1011 câuSome people believe that family is the most important influence on young adults, while others think that friends are more influential. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

🛍️Mua sắm· Shopping

  • Bài 112 câuSome people say that shopping has become a form of entertainment rather than a necessity. Do you think this is a positive or negative development?

  • Bài 314 câuIn many countries, people are buying more goods than they need. What are the causes of this consumerism, and what problems does it cause?

  • Bài 514 câuSome people believe that consumers should be more responsible for the environmental impact of their purchases, while others think that manufacturers should bear this responsibility. Discuss both views and give your opinion.
  • Bài 614 câuNowadays, many people buy things they do not need because of sales and discounts. Why do people do this, and what can be done to help them avoid unnecessary purchases?

  • Bài 1013 câuSome people believe that companies should be prohibited from collecting data about customers' shopping habits. Others think this data helps businesses serve customers better. Discuss both views and give your opinion.

  • Bài 714 câuCrime rates are often higher in cities than in rural areas. Why is this the case, and what can be done to reduce urban crime?

  • Bài 1013 câuSome people think that cities of the future will be very different from today's cities. What changes do you expect to see, and will they be positive or negative?
